## 🛡️ Project Overview

**astrateq-guardian-commerce** is the high-performance e-commerce engine and pre-launch reservation funnel for the **Astra-Guard 8K AI Dashcam**.

Unlike traditional automotive gadgets, this platform is engineered as a **trust-first validation funnel**. It is specifically designed to convert the concerns of the "Sandwich Generation" (adults caring for aging parents and teen drivers) into **$25 refundable deposits**, securing their place in the Astra-Guard ecosystem.

### **Core Promise:** \> *"Earlier signals. Calmer decisions. Safer family drives."*
